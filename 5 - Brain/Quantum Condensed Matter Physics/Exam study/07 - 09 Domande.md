# 07 - 09 Domande
##### 07 - Domande
1. Describe the concept of Parabolic Band Approximation in semiconductor physics and describe the process of photon absorption in semiconductors with graphs.
2. Describe the hamiltonian of a semiconductor perturbed by an electromagnetic wave and derive the first-order time-dependent perturbation theory formula for the transition probability between two states. 
##### 08 - Domande
3. Explain the perturbation theory approach to calculate the optical absorption coefficient in semiconductors. Begin from the perturbed Hamiltonian, derive the coefficients of the wave function, the absorption matrix element, the transition possibility, and the transition rate using Fermi's Golden Rule. 
4. Describe the concept of density of states and joint density of states in semiconductors.
5. Explain the absorption in direct band gap materials, with energies and joint density of states. Do the same for indirect band gap materials, explaining the role of phonons in the process.
6. Describe the concept of excitons in semiconductors, differentiating between Frenkel and Wannier-Mott excitons. Explain their formation, binding energy, and significance in optical properties of semiconductors. And describe bulk optical absorption due to excitons and bulk optical emission.
##### 09 - Domande
7. Explain the concept of F-centers in ionic crystals, how can we describe them, what is their significance in terms of light absorption and emission. Make some examples with different materials and explain how F-centers can be created.
