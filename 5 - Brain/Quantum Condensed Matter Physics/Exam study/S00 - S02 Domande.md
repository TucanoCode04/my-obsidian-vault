# S00 - S02 Domande
##### S00 Domande
1. Describe perfect conductivity and the Meissner effect in superconductors. Describe the critical magnetic field and critical current density in superconductors.
2. Explain the concept of the energy gap in superconductors and how it relates to the superconducting state. What are the main classes of superconductors, and what are their key characteristics?
##### S01 Domande
3. Describe the macroscopic quantum model and the macroscopic Schrödinger equation for superconductors. 
4. Derive the supercurrent density expression from the macroscopic wave function. And its gauge invariance properties.
5. Derive the London equation which describes the response of a superconductor to an external magnetic field. And derive the exponential decay in both field and current density inside a superconductor.
6. Derive the London equation which describes the response of a superconductor to an external electric field. 
##### S02 Domande
7. Explain what is a magnetic flux. Using the supercurrent density expression, derive the quantization of magnetic flux in a superconducting ring.
8. Describe the experimental evidence of magnetic flux quantization in superconducting rings.
