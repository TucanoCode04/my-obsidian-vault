# 06 - 3He Cryostats
There are several methods to achieve temperatures below 1 K, the most common being the use of use of a helium-3 (³He) cryostat and dilution refrigeration.
![[Pasted image 20260106142942.png]]
#### $^3$He Cryostats
The temperature range can be extended down to about 0.3 K by using a helium-3 cryostat. 
The main reasons for using $^3$He are:
- It has a larger vapor pressure at a given temperature compared to $^4$He, allowing for lower temperatures to be achieved through pumping.
