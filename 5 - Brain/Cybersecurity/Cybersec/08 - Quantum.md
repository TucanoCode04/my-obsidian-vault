# 08 - Quantum
**No-Cloning Theorem:**
The no-cloning theorem is a fundamental principle in quantum mechanics that states it is impossible to create an identical copy of an arbitrary unknown quantum state. 
**Quantum Annealing:**
Quantum annealing is a quantum computing method used to find the global minimum of a given objective function over a set of candidate solutions, by exploiting quantum fluctuations. It is particularly useful for solving optimization problems.
